## What this is

Karim's personal hub: the one address every LinkedIn post and episode links to. It's his introduction, portfolio and posts. It runs one email signup, gives each project a page and a waitlist tag once a version of that project is deployed, and grows a "how I built it" section. It belongs to no single project. Hub v1 has no project pages. Packet Village in `karim-belhadjali/packet-village` comes next, then Sila in `karim-belhadjali/islamic-bank`.

The goal and the stages live in the pinned issue [Roadmap: hub](https://github.com/karim-belhadjali/hub/issues/17). Each stage gets one wayfinder map, attached to the roadmap as a sub-issue. The projects' plans are in [Roadmap: Packet Village, from foundations to paid launch](https://github.com/karim-belhadjali/packet-village/issues/1) and [Roadmap: Sila](https://github.com/karim-belhadjali/islamic-bank/issues/21).

Start any session by reading the roadmap, then load the open issue labelled `wayfinder:map` and read its Notes. When a map reaches its destination, close it, tick it on the roadmap, and chart the next stage with `/wayfinder`. Change the plan by editing the roadmap, never inside a map.
