# Hub

Karim's personal site, the one address every post and episode links to. It introduces him, presents each app he builds, and collects people who want to hear when those apps ship.

## Language

**Hub**:
The personal site as a whole, shared by every app Karim builds and owned by none of them.
_Avoid_: Portfolio, blog, homepage

**Project**:
One app Karim builds in public, such as Sila. Each project gets a project page and a waitlist tag.
_Avoid_: Product, app page

**Project page**:
The hub page that presents one project and carries its waitlist signup.
_Avoid_: Landing page, case study

**Post**:
A how-I-built-it article in the hub's posts section, written in English first and translated when Karim chooses.
_Avoid_: Blog post, article, episode

**Episode**:
A video published on LinkedIn about a project. Episodes link to the hub. They are not posts.
_Avoid_: Video post

**Subscriber**:
A person on the hub's single email list. One person is one subscriber, however many projects they wait for.
_Avoid_: Lead, contact, user

**Waitlist**:
The subscribers tagged with one project, such as the Sila waitlist. A filter on the list, never a list of its own.
_Avoid_: Signup list, beta list

**Traction number**:
A count of subscribers, overall or per waitlist, reported to investors from the email service.
_Avoid_: Signups, leads

### Addresses

**Free address**:
The host's own subdomain the hub launches on, before any purchased domain.
_Avoid_: Temporary domain, staging URL

**Custom domain**:
A domain Karim owns, which the hub moves to once it is in hand. Planned as `karimbelhadjali.com`.
_Avoid_: Real domain, production domain

**Domain move**:
Switching the hub from the free address to the custom domain while every link already posted keeps landing.
_Avoid_: Migration, cutover

### Languages

**Locale**:
One language the hub ships in. English, French and Arabic at launch, with room for more.
_Avoid_: Language version, translation

**Fallback**:
Showing the English page when a locale's translation does not exist yet.
_Avoid_: Default language
